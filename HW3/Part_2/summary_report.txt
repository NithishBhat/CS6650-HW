3.You might have observed that one of these is definitely the fastest,
but what are the tradeoffs between these approaches?

Answer:
    Sync.map is the fastest in this scenario by the drawback is that if the reads or write access
    the same addresses then they will be seralized. it excels in read and write to unique addresses.

    Mutex would excel in a scenario of read and write or just write at the same addresses.
    since they would need to be seralized any optimisation would add overhead. it fails to deliver
    in other scenarios where optimizations help.

    RWMutex excels during read heavy scenarios and fails in write heavy ones.
